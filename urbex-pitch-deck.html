The Solution

Our positioning

A game-based city exploration map.

Walking + health + local discovery — through missions, rewards, and story content.

🗺️

Fog-of-war map

Every block you walk reveals a hidden tile. The map becomes a record of your feet.

📖

Curated story POIs

60-second narratives tied to the exact place you're standing. Short stories, not long articles.

🛡️

Fair-play engine

Speed-capped. Privacy-first. Solo-friendly. No forced raids, no surveillance tax.

The discovery moment.

Approach a point of interest — the fog lifts outward in rings from the puck, the tile blooms warm yellow, a pin rises, and a 60-second story of the place unlocks. Built on axial hex coordinates with ring-distance delays.
